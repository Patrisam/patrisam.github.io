About me

I study how language models reason through mathematics.

A model can arrive at the right answer through a faulty chain of reasoning. I’m interested in how to evaluate the intermediate steps and how to choose the examples that models learn from. My collaborators and I explore these questions in EntroCoT.

EntroCoT: Enhancing Chain-of-Thought via Adaptive Entropy-Guided Segmentation

Zihang Li, Yuhang Wang, Yikun Zong, Wenhan Yu, Xiaokun Yuan, Runhan Jiang, Zirui Liu, Tong Yang, and Arthur Jiang

We split a reasoning trace where the model is uncertain, then sample continuations from each prefix to estimate how much a step contributes to the final answer. These estimates help us select traces for fine-tuning.
